# 4. Describe a major outage and how you handled it.

### 請分享一次重大服務中斷，以及你如何處理。

**Primary Leadership Principles：Customer Obsession · Ownership · Bias for Action · Dive Deep**

## S — Situation｜情境

**中文：**
我曾處理一次遊戲正式上線後的掉單P0事件。約5,000人同時在線時服務正常，超過6,000人後開始掉單，接近10,000人時問題快速惡化。

**English：**

I once handled a P0 order-loss incident after a game service went live. The service was normal with about 5,000 concurrent users. After the number went above 6,000, orders began to fail, and the problem became much worse near 10,000 users.

## T — Task｜任務

**中文：**
我的任務是先保護客戶和交易，再找出Root Cause，並且每10分鐘更新一次狀態。

**English：**

My task was to protect customers and transactions first, identify the root cause, and provide a status update every ten minutes.

## O / D — Options and Decision｜選項與決策

**中文：**
我們可以繼續調查、Failover，或Rollback到已驗證版本。新功能不是必要項目，且Rollback可逆，因此我選擇風險較低的Rollback。

**English：**

We could continue investigating, fail over, or roll back to a validated version. The new feature was not essential, and rollback was reversible, so I chose the lower-risk rollback option.

## A — Action｜行動

**中文：**
我立即啟動War Room，分派Infrastructure、Application、Database、Kafka和Customer Communication負責人。我先確認Network、Server、CDN和AWS Status，再檢查Change Record、Release Notes、Deployment Manifest和Configuration Difference。服務恢復後，我沿著Client、Application、Kafka Producer、Consumer和Database建立時間線。每月情境演練也用來驗證告警、升級、Failover、Rollback和通報。

**English：**

I started a war room and assigned owners for infrastructure, application, database, Kafka, and customer communication. I first checked the network, servers, CDN, and AWS status. Then I reviewed the change record, release notes, deployment manifest, and configuration differences. After recovery, I built a timeline across the client, application, Kafka producer, consumer, and database. Our monthly scenario exercises also validate alerting, escalation, failover, rollback, and communication.

## R / L — Result and Learning｜結果與學習

**中文：**
事件發生後45分鐘內恢復上一個已驗證版本。最後確認Kafka容量擴展沒有有效完成，造成Producer寫入Timeout，Retry後也沒有取得ACK。後續加入Kafka Capacity、Producer Error、Partition和Broker Health檢查、Load Test、Scale-out Validation和Rollback Trigger，並在8小時內完成客戶報告。

**English：**

We restored the previous validated version within 45 minutes. We later confirmed that Kafka capacity scaling had not completed correctly. The producer experienced write timeouts, and retries did not receive an acknowledgment. We added checks for Kafka capacity, producer errors, partitions, and broker health, together with load tests, scale-out validation, and rollback triggers. We completed the customer report within eight hours. I learned that fast recovery must be followed by deep analysis and permanent prevention.

# 5. How do you find root cause and use data?

### 你如何找出根本原因並使用數據？

**Primary Leadership Principles：Dive Deep · Are Right, A Lot · Highest Standards**

## S — Situation｜情境

**中文：**
在AXIOM，我負責五個站點。當時MTTD約15分鐘、MTTR約30分鐘，團隊需要更快偵測及恢復。

**English：**

At AXIOM, I was responsible for five sites. Mean Time to Detect, or MTTD, was about 15 minutes, and Mean Time to Restore, or MTTR, was about 30 minutes. The team needed faster detection and recovery.

## T — Task｜任務

**中文：**
我需要先確認監控數據可信，再用Evidence、Failure Mechanism和修正結果確認Root Cause。

**English：**

I needed to verify that the monitoring data was trustworthy first, then confirm the root cause using evidence, a failure mechanism, and the result after correction.

## A — Action｜行動

**中文：**
我建立Observability Standard，定義Metric來源、單位、Timestamp、Sampling Frequency、Aggregation Window、Retention和Owner。我檢查Agent、Exporter、Collector、Alert Pipeline、Missing Sample、Delay、Duplicate和Clock Skew。資料可信後，我用Metrics、Logs、Changes、Network Traffic、Alerts和人員回應建立Incident Timeline。我不把Correlation直接當成Cause，而是提出假設，再透過比較、隔離、Rollback、重現或修正後驗證。每月情境模擬也會測試短時間Peak是否能被發現。

**English：**

I created an observability standard covering metric source, unit, timestamp, sampling frequency, aggregation window, retention, and ownership. I checked the agent, exporter, collector, alert pipeline, missing samples, delays, duplicates, and clock skew. After validating the data, I built an incident timeline using metrics, logs, changes, network traffic, alerts, and human response times. I did not treat correlation as the cause. I created a hypothesis and tested it through comparison, isolation, rollback, reproduction, or verification after a fix. Our monthly scenario exercises also test whether short peaks can be detected.

## R / L — Result and Learning｜結果與學習

**中文：**
我整合Prometheus、Grafana、ELK和PagerDuty，改善告警與Runbook連結。MTTD由15分鐘降至2分鐘，MTTR由30分鐘降至8分鐘。我學到，Data Validation是RCA的第一個技術步驟。

**English：**

I integrated Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, and PagerDuty so alerts could connect to metrics and runbooks. MTTD decreased from 15 minutes to 2 minutes, and MTTR decreased from 30 minutes to 8 minutes. I learned that data validation is the first technical step of root-cause analysis.

# 7. How do you manage a 24x7 data center operations team?

### 你如何管理7×24資料中心營運團隊？

**Primary Leadership Principles：Hire and Develop the Best · Ownership · Earn Trust · Highest Standards**

## Answer — 回答

**中文：**
我會從People、Process、Technology和Metrics四個層面管理7×24團隊。People方面，我建立Skill Matrix，確認每位工程師在Power、UPS、Cooling、Network、Server、Monitoring和Incident Management的能力，再透過Cross-training、Shadow、Reverse Shadow、Critical Procedure Certification和Succession Planning，消除Knowledge Single Point of Failure。

Process方面，我定義Severity、Escalation、Shift Handover、SOP、MOP、EOP、Runbook和Stop Work Authority。Technology方面，我整合Infrastructure、Network、Environmental和Service Monitoring，避免只有大量無法採取行動的Alarm。Metrics方面，我追蹤Availability、MTTD、MTTA、MTTR、Change Failure Rate、Incident Recurrence、Backlog Aging、Capacity和Team Workload。

我每月安排情境演練，模擬Network Ring、Server、Storage、Monitoring失效、Failover失敗或主要負責人不在場。我不先提供答案，而是觀察團隊如何發現、分級、升級、分工和恢復。演練後做Blameless Review，但每個缺口仍指定Owner、期限和重新驗證方式。

**English：**

I manage a 24x7 team through four areas: people, process, technology, and metrics. For people, I create a skill matrix covering power, UPS, cooling, network, servers, monitoring, and incident management. I use cross-training, shadowing, reverse shadowing, critical procedure certification, and succession planning to remove knowledge single points of failure.

For process, I define severity, escalation, shift handover, SOPs, MOPs, EOPs, runbooks, and stop-work authority. For technology, I connect infrastructure, network, environmental, and service monitoring so alerts can lead to action. For metrics, I track availability, MTTD, MTTA, MTTR, change failure rate, incident recurrence, backlog aging, capacity, and team workload.

Every month, I run controlled scenario exercises for a network-ring failure, server or storage failure, missing monitoring alerts, a failed failover, or the absence of a key owner. I do not give the team the full answer in advance. I observe how they detect, classify, escalate, divide the work, and restore service. After each exercise, we hold a blameless review, but every gap still receives an owner, a deadline, and a retest plan.

# 10. How do you prioritize when many tasks are urgent and resources are limited?

### 多項工作同時緊急，但資源有限時如何排序？

**Primary Leadership Principles：Customer Obsession · Ownership · Bias for Action · Deliver Results**

## Answer — 回答

**中文：**
我不會只按FIFO或誰催得最急來排序，而會先降低最高的安全、客戶和營運風險。第一是人員和設施安全；第二是客戶與服務影響；第三是Severity和SLA Risk；接著看Power、Cooling、Network、Security、Dependency、Redundancy、Rollback、備品和Vendor Support。

不同公司的Severity名稱可能不同。以我過去制度為例，最高等級稱為P0；加入Amazon後，我會遵循Amazon的Incident Classification。執行上，我會保留部分Team Capacity處理突發事件，為每項工作指定Owner、下一次更新時間和Escalation Path。如果Blast Radius、客戶影響或恢復選項改變，我會重新排序。

例如一般Provisioning Request和UPS風險同時發生時，我會先處理UPS風險，把具備正確技能的人員投入高風險事件，同時保留交接資訊，讓低風險工作安全恢復。

**English：**

I do not prioritize only by first in, first out or by who is asking the loudest. I first reduce the highest safety, customer, and operational risk. I check personnel and facility safety, customer and service impact, severity and SLA risk, then power, cooling, network, security, dependencies, redundancy, rollback, spare parts, and vendor support.

Severity names can differ between companies. In one of my previous environments, the highest level was called P0. At Amazon, I would follow Amazon’s incident classification. In execution, I reserve part of the team’s capacity for unexpected incidents, assign an owner, a next update time, and an escalation path. If the blast radius, customer impact, or recovery options change, I re-prioritize.

For example, if a normal provisioning request and a UPS risk happen at the same time, I handle the UPS risk first, assign the right skills to it, and preserve handover information so the lower-risk work can resume safely.

**English：**

**English：**

Provisioning decreased from three days to five minutes, and MTTR decreased from 45 minutes to 12 minutes while retaining change tracking, review, and recovery. I learned that quality and speed are not always opposites. A strong operating mechanism can improve both.

# 13. Tell me about a time you made a wrong judgment and what you learned.

### 請分享一次錯誤判斷，以及你從中學到什麼。

**Primary Leadership Principles：Ownership · Dive Deep · Learn and Be Curious · Highest Standards**

## S — Situation｜情境

**中文：**
過去管理資料中心時，我曾經太相信Monitoring Dashboard，將「沒有告警」理解成「沒有異常」。當時部分Metrics使用較長時間區間的Average，短時間CPU、Network或Service Peak被平均值稀釋，Dashboard看起來正常，但現場已經出現服務問題。

**English：**

Earlier in my data center operations experience, I trusted the monitoring dashboard too much. I treated “no alert” as “no abnormality.” Some metrics used an average over a long time window. A short CPU, network, or service peak was diluted by the average, so the dashboard looked normal while the service had a real problem.

## T — Task｜任務

**中文：**
我需要承認監控設計有盲點，找出哪些異常被平均值隱藏，並改善Monitoring、現場確認和Incident Response。

**English：**

I needed to acknowledge the blind spot in the monitoring design, identify which failures were hidden by averages, and improve monitoring, field verification, and incident response.

## A — Action｜行動

**中文：**
我重新檢查Data Source、Sampling Frequency、Aggregation Window、Threshold和Alert Condition。不只看Average，也加入Maximum、P95、P99、Rate of Change、Peak Duration和Consecutive Breaches，並交叉比對OS Counter、Equipment Event、Application Log、Transaction Result和現場狀況。我也要求團隊確認Monitoring Pipeline本身是否健康，不能只依賴單一Dashboard。每月安排異常情境模擬，故意測試短時間Peak、網路異常和服務異常，演練後指定Owner、期限並重新驗證。

**English：**

I reviewed the data source, sampling frequency, aggregation window, thresholds, and alert conditions. We did not look only at averages. We added maximum values, P95 and P99 percentiles, rate of change, peak duration, and consecutive threshold breaches. We also correlated OS counters, equipment events, application logs, transaction results, and field conditions. I required the team to verify that the monitoring pipeline itself was healthy instead of relying on one dashboard. Every month, we ran scenarios for short peaks, network abnormalities, and service failures. After each exercise, we assigned an owner and deadline and retested the fix.

## R / L — Result and Learning｜結果與學習

**中文：**
團隊能看到以前被Average隱藏的短時間Peak，並更早發現設備或服務異常。我學到，Average適合看長期趨勢，但不一定能偵測短時間Failure Mode。Monitoring只是決策工具，仍需搭配Logs、現場觀察和工程判斷。

**English：**

The team could see short peaks that had previously been hidden by averages and detect equipment or service problems earlier. I learned that an average is useful for long-term trends, but it may not detect a short failure mode. Monitoring is a decision tool; it must still be combined with logs, field observation, and engineering judgment.

# 14. What are the biggest challenges in managing a modern data center?

### 你認為管理現代資料中心最大的挑戰是什麼？

**Primary Leadership Principles：Ownership · Think Big · Dive Deep · Highest Standards · Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility**

## Answer — 回答

**中文：**
我認為最大的挑戰不是單一Server，而是Power、Cooling、Capacity、Network、People、Change、Supply Chain和Security彼此相依。資料中心管理不是只看還有多少空Rack，而是要同時確認Power Chain、UPS、PDU、Cooling、Rack Density、Network、Floor Loading、Generator、Fuel、Critical Spare和Vendor Lead Time。

我會建立Failure Mode Matrix，對電力、冷卻、網路、設備和服務定義Primary Signal、Supporting Evidence、Baseline、Failure Criteria、Persistence、Blast Radius、Severity和Response Action。監控方面，我不會只看Average。例如內網平常低於5ms，升到10ms只能先視為Anomaly，還要檢查P95／P99、Packet Loss、Jitter、Route Change和服務影響，才能判斷是否是Incident。

Change方面，我要求MOP、Pre-check、Post-check、Rollback Plan、Approver和Stop Work Authority。People方面，我使用Skill Matrix、Cross-training、Shadow、Reverse Shadow和Critical Procedure Certification，避免Knowledge SPOF。Supply Chain方面，我建立Critical Spare Matrix、Vendor SLA和Replacement Plan。Security方面，我同時考慮IT、OT、BMS、EPMS、UPS Controller、Physical Access和Vendor Remote Access。

我每月安排情境模擬，驗證Monitoring、Incident Command、Failover、Rollback、Vendor Support和Communication。演練後按照Observe、Identify Gaps、Correct和Re-test形成循環。我的管理原則是：在多個彼此耦合的系統中管理風險，同時維持Availability和Operational Discipline。

**English：**

I believe the biggest challenge is not one server. It is the dependency between power, cooling, capacity, network, people, change, supply chain, and security. Data center management is not simply counting empty racks. I need to check the full power chain, UPS, PDUs, cooling, rack density, network, floor loading, generators, fuel, critical spares, and vendor lead times.

I create a failure-mode matrix for power, cooling, network, equipment, and services. It defines the primary signal, supporting evidence, baseline, failure criteria, persistence, blast radius, severity, and response action. For monitoring, I do not look only at averages. For example, if internal network latency is normally below 5 milliseconds and rises to 10 milliseconds, I first treat it as an anomaly. I then check P95 and P99, packet loss, jitter, route changes, and service impact before calling it an incident.

For change management, I require an MOP, pre-checks, post-checks, a rollback plan, an approver, and stop-work authority. For people, I use skill matrices, cross-training, shadowing, reverse shadowing, and critical procedure certification to avoid knowledge single points of failure. For supply chain, I use a critical-spare matrix, vendor SLAs, and replacement plans. For security, I consider IT, OT, BMS, EPMS, UPS controllers, physical access, and vendor remote access.

Every month, I run scenario exercises to validate monitoring, incident command, failover, rollback, vendor support, and communication. After each exercise, we follow a cycle of observe, identify gaps, correct, and retest. My management principle is to manage risk across coupled systems while maintaining availability and operational discipline.
