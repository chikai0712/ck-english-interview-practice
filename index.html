IT Vocabulary

IT 技術專有名詞

先熟悉工作中最常出現的名詞，再練習用完整句子說明。

TERM 01

ERP — Enterprise Resource Planning

企業資源規劃：管理財務、採購、庫存及企業營運流程。

例句：We integrated ERP with the production and warehouse systems.

TERM 02

MES — Manufacturing Execution System

製造執行系統：追蹤工單、在製品、設備狀態及生產結果。

例句：MES gives the factory real-time production information.

TERM 03

Data Platform

數據平台：收集、儲存及整理不同系統的資料，供報表與分析使用。

例句：We built a data platform for production and business dashboards.

TERM 04

API — Application Programming Interface

應用程式介面：讓兩個系統以受控方式交換資料。

例句：The API connects the CRM system with ERP.

TERM 05

IT/OT Integration

IT／OT 整合：連接企業系統、工廠設備及工業網路。

例句：IT and OT integration must protect production stability.

TERM 06

Edge Gateway

邊緣閘道器：在靠近產線的位置收集設備資料，再將需要的資訊送到企業或雲端系統。

例句：The Edge Gateway collects equipment data without changing the control loop.

TERM 07

Management Dashboard

管理儀表板：將重要的業務或系統資訊集中呈現，協助管理階層快速決策。

例句：The dashboard shows production progress, inventory, and quality issues.

TERM 08

DevOps

DevOps：結合開發、維運、自動化及監控，讓軟體更快且更穩定地交付。

例句：DevOps helps the team release changes with better control.

TERM 09

RTO / RPO — Recovery Time / Point Objective

復原時間目標／復原點目標：定義系統要在多久內恢復，以及可接受多少資料遺失。

例句：We set RTO and RPO for critical factory and business systems.

TERM 10

MTTD / MTTR — Mean Time to Detect / Recover

平均偵測時間／平均修復時間：衡量團隊發現及修復事件的速度。

例句：Better monitoring helped us reduce MTTD and MTTR.

Interview Questions

常見面試題目與回答

回答先求清楚，再補充實際經驗；每段英文都可以播放練習。

INTERVIEW 01

Q: Could you tell me about yourself?
A: I have more than 20 years of experience in IT, technology architecture, and team management. My background includes manufacturing, online services, cloud platforms, and cybersecurity.

中文問題：請介紹一下你自己。
中文回答：我在 IT、技術架構及團隊管理方面有二十多年的經驗，經歷涵蓋製造業、線上服務、雲端平台及資安。

INTERVIEW 02

Q: What are your main strengths?
A: My main strengths are system integration, technical planning, team management, and turning ideas into practical projects.

中文問題：你主要的優點或專長是什麼？
中文回答：我的主要專長是系統整合、技術規劃、團隊管理，以及將想法轉成可以執行的專案。

INTERVIEW 03

Q: What is one area you are still improving?
A: I sometimes spend too much time checking details in high-risk projects. I now set clear priorities and deadlines to keep a balance between quality and speed.

中文問題：你目前還在改善哪一方面？
中文回答：在高風險專案中，我有時會花較多時間確認細節。現在我會先設定優先順序與期限，在品質與速度之間取得平衡。

INTERVIEW 04

Q: Why are you interested in this IT Manager position?
A: I am interested because the role combines IT operations, system integration, cybersecurity, and digital improvement. These areas match my experience.

中文問題：你為什麼對這個 IT Manager 職位有興趣？
中文回答：因為這個職位結合 IT 營運、系統整合、資安及數位改善，這些方向與我的經驗相符。

INTERVIEW 05

Q: How do you manage a difficult project?
A: I first confirm the business goal, the current situation, and the main risks. Then I set priorities, assign responsibilities, and track progress with the team.

中文問題：你如何管理一個困難的專案？
中文回答：我會先確認業務目標、現況及主要風險，再設定優先順序、分配責任，並與團隊追蹤進度。

INTERVIEW 06

Q: How do you work with people from different departments?
A: I listen to their needs first, explain the technical impact in simple language, and agree on a practical plan with clear owners and deadlines.

中文問題：你如何與不同部門的人合作？
中文回答：我會先了解對方需求，用簡單的方式說明技術影響，再一起訂出有負責人及期限的可行方案。

INTERVIEW 07

Q: Tell me about a project you are proud of.
A: I helped build a manufacturing data platform. It integrated production lines, barcode scanners, MES, ERP, and warehouse data into dashboards for production and business decisions.

中文問題：請分享一個你比較有成就感的專案。
中文回答：我曾協助建置製造數據平台，整合產線、條碼掃描器、MES、ERP 及倉庫資料，並產出支援生產與營運決策的儀表板。

INTERVIEW 08

Q: How do you handle a production incident?
A: I focus first on service and production stability. Then I organize the response team, confirm the impact, communicate clearly, and complete a root cause review after recovery.

中文問題：你如何處理正式環境或生產線事件？
中文回答：我會先確保服務與生產穩定，再組織應變團隊、確認影響、清楚溝通，並在恢復後完成根因檢討。

INTERVIEW 09

Q: How do you decide whether to build or buy a solution?
A: I compare business value, time to market, total cost, internal skills, security, and long-term maintenance before making a recommendation.

中文問題：你如何決定自製系統或購買現成方案？
中文回答：我會比較商業價值、上市時間、總成本、內部能力、資安及長期維護，再提出建議。

INTERVIEW 10

Q: What would you do in your first 90 days?
A: I would listen to the business and technical teams, review the current products and projects, identify the main gaps, and propose a clear roadmap with a few practical priorities.

中文問題：你上任前 90 天會做什麼？
中文回答：我會先了解業務與技術團隊，盤點目前產品及專案，找出主要差距，再提出幾個可執行的優先事項與清楚藍圖。

INTERVIEW 11

Q: What are your salary expectations?

中文問題：你的期望薪資是多少？

INTERVIEW 12
