01

Good morning, my name is CK Chiu. Thank you for giving me this opportunity.

早安，我是 CK Chiu。謝謝您給我這次面試機會。

02

I have more than 20 years of experience in IT, technology architecture, and team management. My background includes manufacturing, online services, cloud platforms, and information security.

我在 IT、技術架構及團隊管理方面有二十多年的經驗。過去的工作經歷涵蓋製造業、線上服務、雲端平台及資訊安全。

03

In my previous roles, I managed IT and technology teams across different countries. I was responsible for IT infrastructure, enterprise systems, cloud services, cybersecurity, and digital transformation projects.

在過去的工作中，我曾管理跨國 IT 及技術團隊，負責 IT 基礎架構、企業系統、雲端服務、網路安全及數位轉型專案。

04

I have experience working with ERP, MES, WMS, CRM, factory equipment, data platforms, and management dashboards. I also worked with business, research and development, manufacturing, and IT teams to move projects from planning to implementation.

我熟悉 ERP、MES、WMS、CRM、工廠設備、數據平台及管理儀表板等系統。我也曾與業務、研發、製造及 IT 團隊合作，將專案從規劃推進到實際執行。

05

One of my main strengths is turning a technology plan into a project that can be executed. I usually start by understanding the business needs. Then I define the system architecture, evaluate the technology, run a proof of concept, and support testing, pilot production, and go-live.

我的主要專長之一，是將技術規劃轉化為可以執行的專案。我通常會先了解業務需求，再定義系統架構、評估技術方案、進行概念驗證，並協助測試、試產及正式上線。

06

In manufacturing, I helped build a production data platform. We integrated data from production lines, barcode scanners, MES, WMS, equipment, and other systems. The data was used for automation, production tracking, exception monitoring, and management reports.

在製造業方面，我曾協助建置生產數據平台，整合產線、條碼掃描器、MES、WMS、設備及其他系統的資料。這些資料應用於自動化、生產追蹤、異常監控及管理報表。

07

I also used the same approach to integrate CRM, ERP, and other business systems. We built real-time dashboards to help managers understand production progress, equipment status, inventory, quality issues, and overall business operations.

我也運用相同的整合方式，串接 CRM、ERP 及其他企業系統，建立即時管理儀表板，協助管理階層掌握生產進度、設備狀況、庫存、品質問題及整體營運情形。

08

I am comfortable working with different technical teams and business departments. I can help set up a common architecture, define priorities, and create a clear evaluation process for new technologies and products.

我習慣與不同的技術團隊及業務部門合作。我可以協助建立共同的技術架構、訂定工作優先順序，並為新技術及新產品建立清楚的評估流程。

09

Based on my understanding, JYT has strong experience in PC modules, sensing technology, PCBA, optical products, and manufacturing. I believe I can help connect these existing capabilities with data, software, automation, and new technology applications.

依照我目前的了解，建皇光電在 PC 模組、感測技術、PCBA、光學產品及製造方面都有良好的基礎。我相信自己可以協助將這些既有能力，與數據、軟體、自動化及新技術應用結合起來。

10

I am not an expert in every technology area, but I am experienced in organizing experts, connecting different teams, and making projects move forward. I hope to use my experience to support JYT's technology roadmap and help turn new ideas into tested and practical products.

我不可能是每個技術領域的專家，但我有組織專業人才、整合不同團隊及推動專案進展的經驗。我希望運用過去的經驗，協助建皇光電建立技術發展藍圖，並將新的想法轉化為經過驗證、可以實際應用的產品。

11

Thank you.

謝謝您。
